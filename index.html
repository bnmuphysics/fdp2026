About the programme

Equipping teachers for the digital and AI era

Rapid advances in Information and Communication Technology and Artificial Intelligence are changing teaching, learning, research and governance in higher education. This six-day programme gives faculty members practical skills in digital pedagogy, learning management systems, AI-assisted teaching and research tools, prompt engineering, and the responsible and ethical use of AI — with hands-on practice on their own laptops in almost every session.

What you will learn

  • National digital platforms: SWAYAM, NPTEL, e-PG Pathshala, NDLI, Shodhganga, Vidwan, SAMARTH
  • Foundations of AI, machine learning and generative AI — without heavy mathematics
  • Prompt engineering for teaching, research and administration
  • Interactive content, digital assessment and Google Classroom / Moodle
  • AI for research, academic writing, curriculum and outcome mapping
  • Academic integrity, AI ethics, data privacy (DPDP Act, 2023) and AI policy

Who should attend

  • Faculty members of all disciplines — Science, Social Sciences, Humanities, Commerce and others
  • From the University Departments and Constituent Colleges of BNMU
  • Priority to early-career faculty and those due for CAS promotion

Highlights

  • Experts from IIT, NIT and reputed Universities and Institutes
  • Hands-on training on your own laptop · Medium of instruction: English
  • Working lunch and tea on all six days · Certificate on completion

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who can apply?

Faculty members of all disciplines from the University Departments and Constituent Colleges of B. N. Mandal University.

What is the last date of registration?

Is the NOC compulsory?

Yes. Upload the NOC signed by your Principal (colleges) or Head of the Department (University Departments) as a PDF in the registration form.

Do I need my own laptop?

Yes. Almost every session has hands-on work, which you will do on your own laptop. Please bring it charged every day.

I am not from a science background. Will I be able to follow?

Yes. The programme is designed for teachers of all disciplines. Concepts are explained without heavy mathematics or programming, using free and easy tools.

How do I get the certificate?

Certificates are issued to participants with at least 90% attendance and at least 50% marks in the final MCQ assessment, as per the MMTTC guidelines.

Will lunch be provided?

Yes. Working lunch and tea are provided on all six days.

How will I know if I am selected?

Selected participants will be informed by email / WhatsApp. The list on this website is updated automatically from the registration form.
